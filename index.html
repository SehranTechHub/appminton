Built by someone who plays — for people who play

Appminton wasn't dreamt up in a boardroom. It was built by a badminton player who got tired of the same Saturday-morning routine: chasing payments in the group chat, counting shuttles on a notepad, and doing the maths on who owes what after every session.

If you play because you love the game — the rallies, the regulars, the after-game teh tarik — then this was made for you. The goal was simple: take care of the boring admin so you can spend more time on court and less time being the group's accountant.

— Made with passion, by a player who shows up week after week, same as you. 🏸

Features

Everything your group needs

From booking to settlement — Appminton handles the details so you can focus on the game.

🏸

Session Scheduling

Create sessions with court details, time slots, and player capacity. Invite friends and group members in seconds.

🪙

Fair Payment Splitting

Automatically calculate who owes what. Supports flat splits, gender-based rates, and shuttle-usage billing.

🏹

Shuttle Tracking

Log shuttle consumption in real time during play. Costs are distributed proportionally to attendance.

Group Management

Organise regular players into groups. Invite the whole group to a session with a single tap.

Push, email, and in-app notifications keep every player updated — from invite to payment confirmation.

⚡

Real-time Updates

WebSocket-powered live updates so changes to sessions and payments reflect instantly for all players.

How it works

From invite to settled in minutes

A simple flow that works for casual groups and competitive regulars alike.

1

Create a session

Set the date, venue, court fee, and maximum players. Choose your payment mode — flat, by gender, or by shuttle usage.

2

Invite your players

Send invites to friends or entire groups. Players accept, decline, or KIV directly from the app.

3

Track the session

Mark attendance, log shuttles used, and update the session as it progresses. Everything is recorded in real time.

4

Settle up

Appminton calculates the optimal settlement — minimum transactions, maximum fairness. One tap to confirm payments.
