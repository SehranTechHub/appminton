Privacy Policy for Appminton

Last updated: 22 May 2026

Appminton respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the Appminton mobile application and related services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information reasonably necessary to operate the app and provide badminton session management features.

Account Information

  • Name or nickname
  • Email address or phone number used for login

Session & Group Information

  • Groups you create or join
  • Sessions you create or participate in
  • Attendance records
  • Scores and match results
  • Ranking and match classification information
  • Session history

Ranking Information

Where voluntarily provided by users, gender information may be used solely for badminton match classification and ranking calculations, including men’s doubles, women’s doubles, and mixed doubles game formats.

Cost & Payment Information

  • Court booking costs
  • Shuttlecock usage and cost allocations
  • Payment responsibilities between players
  • Payment statuses such as pending or paid
  • Serve credit transaction records and balances

Technical Information

  • Device type
  • App version
  • Crash and error logs
  • Basic diagnostic information

2. Information We Do Not Collect

  • Contacts from your phone
  • Precise location or background location tracking
  • Camera or microphone recordings unless explicitly initiated by the user
  • Personal files outside the app
  • Banking passwords or payment card details

3. Payments

Payments are processed through third-party payment providers such as Apple App Store and Google Play. Appminton does not store your bank account details, payment card numbers, or e-wallet credentials.

We may receive payment confirmations, transaction references, and purchase status information.

4. Serve Credit Transactions

We collect and store information related to Serve credit purchases, balances, and usage for the purpose of enabling session creation and maintaining account records.

Serve credits are digital usage entitlements and not stored monetary balances.

5. How We Use Your Information

  • Create and manage badminton sessions
  • Display attendance, scores, rankings, and session history
  • Calculate and display cost breakdowns
  • Display payment responsibilities
  • Operate ranking and ELO-related features
  • Improve app performance and reliability
  • Provide customer support
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, or misuse of the app

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data.

Your information may be shared:

  • With other players within the same group or session where relevant to app functionality
  • With trusted service providers required to operate the app
  • Where required by law or legal process

7. Data Retention

We retain your information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

For details on account deletion, data deletion, anonymization, and retained records, please refer to our Account Deletion Policy.

8. Your Rights

  • Access your personal data
  • Update or correct your information
  • Request deletion of your account and data
  • Withdraw consent where applicable

9. Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, including encryption and secure servers, to help protect user information.

However, no system can guarantee absolute security.

10. Children’s Privacy

Appminton is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted within the app or on the relevant policy page.
