Features

Take a closer look at
Appminton

A guided tour of everything Appminton does for your badminton group — from scheduling sessions to settling up, ranking players, and running the books.

Explore Features Back to home
🏸 Session Scheduling

Set up a session in seconds

Create sessions with court details, time slots, and player capacity, then invite friends and group members in a couple of taps.

  • Pick venue, date, time, and court fee
  • Set a player cap and let people accept, decline, or KIV
  • Invite individuals or a whole group at once
🪙 Fair Payment Splitting

Everyone pays their fair share

Appminton works out who owes what automatically, then suggests the simplest set of settlements so nobody's stuck doing maths in the group chat.

  • Four modes: flat, flat by gender, shuttle usage, shuttle + gender
  • Optimal settlement — fewest transactions, maximum fairness
  • One tap to mark a payment as settled
🏹 Shuttle Tracking

Count shuttles, not headaches

Log shuttle consumption in real time during play, and let Appminton spread the cost proportionally across everyone who turned up.

  • Tap to add shuttles as the session goes
  • Costs distributed by attendance, automatically
  • No more guessing how many tubes you went through
👥 Group Management

Keep your regulars in one place

Organise your regular players into groups so getting a session together is as easy as a single tap.

  • Build groups of your usual playing crew
  • Invite the whole group to a session instantly
  • Members stay in sync across every session
📊 Ranking & Analysis

See how everyone's really playing

Every scored session feeds a detailed analysis and an ELO-based group leaderboard, so your community can track form, rivalries, and bragging rights over time.

  • Session analysis for every scored session
  • ELO-based ranking system for the group leaderboard
  • Insights that show trends across your sessions
📈 Organizer Dashboard

Run the books with confidence

Get an organizer's-eye view of every session you've run — see the money side at a glance and know exactly where things stand.

  • Overview of all the sessions you've organized
  • Profit and loss per session, clearly broken down
  • Track how much has been collected and what's outstanding
