Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly will I get a response?

We aim to respond to all inquiries within 24 hours during business days. For urgent matters, please mark your message as such.

What should I do if I found a bug?

Please send a detailed description of the bug to support@appminton.com along with steps to reproduce it. Include your device type and app version if possible.

Are you accepting partnership proposals?

Yes! If you're interested in partnerships, sponsorships, or collaborations, please reach out to support@appminton.com with details about your proposal.

How can I request a new feature?

We'd love to hear your suggestions! Please contact us at support@appminton.com with your feature request and explain how it would benefit the Appminton community.

What languages do you support?

Our support team primarily communicates in English and Malaysian (Bahasa Melayu). However, we'll do our best to assist you in your preferred language.

Response Time & Expectations

Our support team operates during standard business hours (Monday – Friday, 9 AM – 6 PM MYT). For urgent issues outside these hours, please send your message and we'll respond as soon as possible.
