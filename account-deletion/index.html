Account Management

Account Deletion

Last updated: 5 May 2026

This page explains how to delete your Appminton account. Account deletion is a permanent action that removes all your personal data from our system (within 30 days). Please review the following information carefully before proceeding.

How to Delete Your Account

You can delete your account directly from the Appminton mobile application. Follow these steps:

  1. Open the Appminton mobile app
  2. Navigate to Settings (gear icon)
  3. Select Account from the menu
  4. Scroll down to the bottom and tap Delete Account
  5. You will be asked to confirm your decision. Enter your password or authenticate using biometric verification
  6. Tap Confirm Deletion
  7. Your account will be deleted, and you will be logged out automatically

Once deletion is initiated, your account cannot be recovered. A confirmation email will be sent to your registered email address.

Alternative: Request Deletion via Email

If you prefer to request account deletion without using the app, you can contact our support team directly:

Email: support@appminton.com

Please include your registered email address in your request. We will process your deletion request within 7 business days.

What Gets Deleted

When your account is deleted, the following data is permanently removed within 30 days:

  • Your account profile (name, email, profile picture)
  • Your personal settings and preferences
  • Your session history and attendance records
  • Game scores and personal statistics
  • Your Serve credit balance (non-refundable)
  • Your push notification preferences and device tokens
  • Any groups you created (if you are the sole member)

What Is NOT Deleted

Certain information may persist in our system for operational, legal, or financial reasons:

  • Session records: Historical sessions you participated in may remain in the database to maintain the integrity of other players' session records (names are anonymized)
  • Payment records: Transactions and settlement records are retained for financial auditing and tax compliance purposes
  • Audit logs: Anonymized activity logs may be retained for security and abuse prevention

No personal identifiable information will be associated with these retained records.

⚠️ Important: Account deletion is permanent and irreversible. Once deleted, you cannot recover your account, session history, or Serve credits. If you created sessions that are still ongoing, you may wish to transfer ownership to another organizer before deleting your account.

Impact on Groups and Sessions

Groups you created: If you are the sole organizer of a group, the group may be deactivated or transferred based on our policies.

Sessions you organized: Ongoing or future sessions will be affected. We recommend transferring session management responsibilities to another organizer before deleting your account.

Sessions you joined: Your attendance records will be anonymized, but the session will remain in other players' histories.

Data Deletion Timeline

  • Immediate: Your account is disabled and you are logged out. The app will no longer recognize your credentials.
  • Within 24 hours: Your personal data is queued for deletion from our primary database
  • Within 30 days: All personal data is permanently deleted from our systems and backups
  • Note: Due to database backup cycles, complete removal may take up to 30 days

Before You Delete

Please consider the following before deleting your account:

  • Ensure any outstanding payment obligations are settled (although anonymized records will remain)
  • Download any important information you wish to retain (we do not provide data export features)
  • Transfer ownership of any groups or sessions you manage
  • Inform fellow players if they need to contact you before deletion
  • This action is permanent and cannot be undone

Restrictions on Re-registration

After deleting your account, you may register a new account using the same email address after a 30-day period. This is to ensure all data is completely purged from our system.

Privacy and Compliance

Account deletion is conducted in compliance with Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA). We are committed to respecting your right to erasure under applicable data protection laws. For more information, see our Privacy Policy.

Need Help?

If you have questions about account deletion or need assistance, please contact our support team:

Email: support@appminton.com

We will respond to your inquiry within 24 hours.
