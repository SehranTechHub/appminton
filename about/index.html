About Us

Building Software That Solves Real Problems

Appminton Technology — practical digital products for communities, businesses, and the future.

Appminton Technology is a Malaysian software company focused on creating practical digital solutions that improve how people organize, collaborate, and engage with their communities.

We believe that great software should solve real-world problems in a simple and intuitive way. Rather than building technology for the sake of technology, we focus on developing products that remove friction from everyday activities and help people spend more time doing what they enjoy.

Our journey began with a simple observation: badminton groups often rely on multiple tools, spreadsheets, chat applications, and manual processes to organize sessions, manage costs, track attendance, and coordinate players. As badminton enthusiasts ourselves, we saw an opportunity to create a better solution.

This led to the creation of Appminton, our flagship product.

Appminton

Appminton is designed to be the operating system for badminton groups.

The platform helps players and organizers manage badminton sessions, attendance, rankings, costs, and payments in a single place. By bringing these workflows together, Appminton enables badminton groups to spend less time managing logistics and more time enjoying the sport.

Our vision is simple:

When people think about organizing badminton, they should think about Appminton.
