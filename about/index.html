About

Built by a Badminton Player, for Badminton Players

Appminton — the operating system for badminton groups.

Appminton is a Malaysian-built app that helps badminton groups organize sessions, track attendance and rankings, and settle costs — all in one place.

Appminton started with a simple observation: badminton groups juggle spreadsheets, chat groups, payment screenshots, and manual reminders just to get a session running. Someone always ends up chasing payments, recounting who showed up, or rewriting the same message every week.

I play badminton myself and ran into that friction every week — so I built the tool I wished existed.

What Appminton Does

Appminton brings everything a badminton group needs into a single app:

  • Create and share sessions, and let players confirm their spots
  • Track attendance automatically, session after session
  • Split court and shuttle costs fairly, without the guesswork
  • Keep payments visible so organizers stop chasing people
  • Follow rankings and match history across your group

The goal is straightforward: less time on logistics, more time on court.

The vision is simple:

When people think about organizing badminton, they should think about Appminton.

The Mission

To make organizing badminton effortless, so groups can play more and manage less.

Behind Appminton

Appminton is an independent app built and maintained in Malaysia under Appminton Technology. It's a one-person project with a single focus: the Appminton app. I play the sport, I run my own sessions, and I use the app every week alongside the groups it's built for.

What Guides the App
